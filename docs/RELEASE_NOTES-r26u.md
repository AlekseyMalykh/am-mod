# Candidate r26u — pad lighting roles

Local unpublished device candidate. Five assignable hues (Blue, Violet, Magenta,
Orange, Pink) plus Default; stable archived IDs, read-only legacy hue mapping.
Service colours are excluded from Pad/Sequence Colour controls. Source selections
flash white twice; target grids show occupied red/free green, with a same-colour
cursor. Sample gets a white focus marker and grey muted-event flashes without
changing audio/mute state. Native Chop follows source identity with a white marker.
Steps deletion warnings outrank playhead/selection. Accepted Seq timing, K fixes,
OptoCat/Warm, gain limits, EQ sidebars and experimental FAT32 are preserved.

See the [Russian release notes](RELEASE_NOTES-r26u.ru.md) for exact coverage,
legacy palette mapping and downgrade limitations. Separate Loop/Song markers and
complete native Erase/FX unification remain proposed; hardware checks are pending.
