# r29zzo

- Tuner window over Sample mode: hold Stop and press Chop. Every pad you hit shows the pitch of its sample (note, cents, Hz, pad name). Any of B1/B2/B3/Chop closes it.
- The tuner window looks the same in the Mixer (Input > ROUTE), Sample Record (B3) and Sample mode.
- Sample Record: the "Tuner" label is lower and no longer covers the threshold icon.
- Not tested on a device yet: reading the selected pad's PCM on the model queue (log lines TUNER_SAMPLE) and the pitch result on real samples.
